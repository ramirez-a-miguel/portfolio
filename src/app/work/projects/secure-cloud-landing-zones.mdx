## Overview

At Deloitte Consulting GmbH, Miguel worked in the Cloud Transformation & Systems Engineering practice on enterprise cloud solutions and infrastructure modernization for customers in the DACH region.

## Focus Areas

- **Secure landing zones**: Designed and delivered landing zones aligned with BSI IT-Grundschutz and BSI C5 standards.
- **Sovereign cloud proof of concept**: Partnered with AWS and Landing Zone Accelerator teams on a public-sector proof of concept incorporating confidential computing.
- **Operational documentation**: Created architecture documentation, runbooks, and playbooks to standardize cloud operations.
- **DevSecOps and FinOps**: Built CI/CD pipelines and implemented Cloud FinOps reporting with Txture to support cost transparency and operational control.
- **Team enablement**: Supported nearshore onboarding and introduced low-code automation to streamline landing zone deployment.
